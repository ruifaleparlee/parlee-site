ASSESSMENT

Six dimensions. The full picture.

Every session is scored on six axes covering the full cycle of a sales negotiation.

01Needs discovery

Do you find the real problem behind the objection?

02Objection handling

Do you respond precisely, or give in too early?

03Value creation

Does the client see what they gain, or only what they pay?

04Concessions

Does every concession get something back, or is it given away?

05Moving the deal forward

Does the conversation move towards close, or go round in circles?

06Rapport

Do you build trust, or push without connecting?
